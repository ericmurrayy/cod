Quick answers

Before you call

My spring broke. Can I still use the door?+

No. The spring carries the door's full weight. Using the opener or lifting it by hand with a broken spring is how doors and backs get destroyed. Leave it alone and call.

What does a repair cost?+

Describe the problem on the phone and we'll give you a realistic range before anything is scheduled. On site, you approve a written quote before work starts. No surprise line items.

How fast can you get here?+

Trucks dispatch from Chelmsford, so most calls in our core towns get a same-day window. Call and we'll tell you honestly when we can be there.

Do you service doors you didn't install?+

Every make, every model, residential and commercial, regardless of who installed it.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes. Fully licensed and insured for residential and commercial work in Massachusetts. Happy to walk you through it on the phone.

How long does a spring replacement take?+

Usually under an hour once we're on site. If both springs are the same age, we'll recommend replacing the pair. They wear together, and it saves you a second service call.

Can you install a door or opener I bought myself?+

We can. We'll look the hardware over first and tell you straight if it's not right for your opening, then install it the same way we'd install our own.
